## 1. Abstract

- The instrument claim: a domain language (program → coordinates via
  `build`; coordinates → program via `lift`) with measured round-trip
  fidelity 5.00e-4 (the print half-quantum) over 78,078 lifts.
- The physics claim: in field-aligned dipole order of a confined LiPF6
  electrolyte, detected blind (lift never told the field) at pre-registered
  Welch tests; filling dependence handled honestly (pilot vs open-tube
  uptake; Q3 voided; R13 at the measured uptake NOT CONFIRMED, p 0.59).
- Limits stated up front: pilot force field, packing-based fillings,
  narrow-tube non-ergodicity, cluster FP non-reproducibility, n = 6/field.

## 2. Introduction

- Problem: simulation studies are prose + scripts; no machine-readable
  statement of what a configuration IS (macrostate, not microstate).
- The Chaord move: a language whose sentences are macrostate statements
  (six kinds: build/state/constrain/assert/history/conserve); compile =
  realize coordinates; decompile = lift a frame to a program.
- Why an instrument claim first (Gate A'/acceptance culture, A1-A14) and
  why the physics claim is second: the language is only as good as its
  measured fidelity.

## 8. What the language adds beyond the physics (discussion)

- Lift as an instrument: the field was detected through the lifted
  programs, never from coordinates with field knowledge.
- Conservation and residual accounting as falsifiable statements
  (`src/chaord/check/`, "drop one atom" mutations fail).
- KA-glass and LJ-liquid convergence studies (X7 items 1-2:
  `research/ka_convergence/` — IS-energy decades resolved (+0.040/+0.023,
  CI 6-10x), structure not; `research/x7_liquid/` — bootstrap CIs,
  lj_liquid tightened x0.92/0.93, cn_tv widened x1.37/1.57) as the
  measurement-error model for observables.

## 9. Limitations (T5c; D57 list, verbatim)

1. Pilot force field (OPLS-AA rigid + Werder carbon, LB mixing deviation) —
   no DFT anchor yet (owner question 3, owner_inputs.md).
2. Filling: packed-pore constructions; the reservoir-filling runs come from
   a fill relaxation, and R13 packs the measured uptake — not a grand-canonical
   ensemble.
3. Narrow-tube non-ergodicity: (10,10) is glassy-slow (tau_rot); block
   protocol sized by tau_M (2 x 3.01 ns) may still under-sample.
4. Cluster floating-point non-reproducibility: cross-build chaining checked
   (identical thermo at ladder t=0) but reruns are not bit-reproducible.
5. 6 runs per field: detection is a Welch call at alpha = 0.05 with D48
   95% CIs; power against small shifts is limited (Q3's lesson, re-learned
   at n = 3).
